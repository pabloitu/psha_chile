# X3 figures

- `figs/x3_gccel_map.png`: GCCEL events in the box coloured by depth (size by magnitude), with cluster names and the flat slab shaded; depth against latitude.
- `figs/x3_diff_by_depth.png`: epicentre distance and depth difference (measured depths) to GCCEL per source, by GCCEL depth range.
- `figs/x3_potin_ehb_depth.png`: Potin, ISC-EHB L1 and L2 depth minus GCCEL depth against GCCEL depth (≥ 40 km), in and outside the flat slab.
- `figs/x3_variant_scores.png`: mean epicentre distance and mean |Δz| against GCCEL per location variant, by GCCEL depth range.
