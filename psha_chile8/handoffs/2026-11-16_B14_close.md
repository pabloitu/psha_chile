## Traps
- Plain `python` is not on the PATH. Use the full path of the `psha_renta` interpreter above.
- `geopandas.read_file` on the fault gpkg fails with pyogrio, because GDAL_DATA is incomplete. Use `engine="fiona"` and `layer="faults"`.
- The catalogue's two nodal planes are in no fixed order. Do not type or average on `strike1` alone without the plane choice in `representative()`.
- `moved()` pairs the rows of each table in TYPES order. It finds the kept types from that date's shares and counts tables, so it needs both tables of `PREV` on disk.
- The Patagonia fault mechanism (110/90/0) matches none of its 7 mechanisms (Kagan angles 53–95°). This is accepted and written in `REPORT_B14.md`. Do not read it as a fit to the events.
- `npd.py` raises a KeyError if a class without a `FAULT` entry falls below 15 weighted mechanisms. Today only the backarc and Patagonia do.
