## What A2 owns
- `catalog/fetch.py`, `catalog/read.py`, `catalog/profile.py` (settings at the top). From `psha_chile8/catalog/`, in order:
  - `/data/miniforge3/envs/psha_renta/bin/python fetch.py`: downloads into `sources/raw/<name>/` with `fetch.json`. It resumes and skips files already on disk. ISC takes hours, CSN web about 1 h and ComCat about 30 min; they can run in parallel terminals (`python -c "import fetch; fetch.isc()"`). - Tests: `catalog/tests/test_read.py` (27), real samples in `catalog/tests/data/<source>/`. Run `pytest catalog/tests` from `psha_chile8/`.

## Traps
- A refreeze needs a new download date. `read.py` names its tables after the `downloaded` date in `fetch.json`, so rerunning it on the same raw files overwrites the `2026-11-10` tables and their MANIFEST rows. Everything downstream reads those names.
- ComCat event IDs change between downloads, and the ComCat counts change too; only the frozen files are reproducible. Without `sources/raw/` on disk, the exact tables cannot be rebuilt.
- Four ISC monthly files end with a stray `?` after `</q:quakeml>`; `quakeml()` cuts at the closing tag.
- The ISC box search returns a few events with no origin in the box. `inbox()` drops them, and every reader except rm2018 must call it.
- ComCat moment tensors:
  - before 1996 they are focal mechanisms only, with no moment;
  - older products have M0 but no derived Mw. `profile.py` computes Mw from M0 for the figure only; the tables keep it empty;
  - the Maule 2010 WCMT scalar moment is wrong.
- ComCat preferred depths of 10 / 33 / 35 km carry no fixed flag.
- Potin has a gap from February to August 2000.
- `import profile` in `catalog/` shadows the standard-library `profile` module; run the script from `catalog/`.
