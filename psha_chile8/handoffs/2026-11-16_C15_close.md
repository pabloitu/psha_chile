## Open
- **Zone bottom 95 % or 99 %.** Proposed: keep 95 % (0.475). The 99 % fraction is 0.713, driven by catalogue tails (Riedel-Hornig 0.553). The 0.65 option holds Chillán 1939 and 1971-05-08 and is already built. Not confirmed by Pablo.
- **Heavy top slice where the slab top is 50–70 km deep** (w1 0.50 against about 0.26), where the interface band fades. It may be the down-dip end of the interface counted as in-slab.
- **For A7 / CAT:** the Haynie crustal ramp (± 20 km about the slab top) gives forearc wedge events small in-slab shares. In the depth model this is about 9 000 events with a total weight of 240. They are also in the rates, a small effect at Mw ≥ 4.5.
- **South of 36° S:** few events; the weights there come mostly from the kernel.
- **Not done:** Beck et al. (1998) and the Tarapacá papers are not in `refs/`. LEARNINGS proposals are in REPORT_C15 sections 5 and 8.

- `depth.CAT` is hard-coded to catalogue 16c. CURRENT.md lists a newer frozen catalogue (16e) that the models do not use yet, on purpose.
- The three-layer columns of `depth_cells_2026-11-16d.csv` differ from the S2 reference (class weights, nest fix, slicing fix). Don't mix them into the reference branch.
- Runs 2026-11-16b and 16c are superseded the same day: 16b read catalogue 2026-11-16, and 16c had weight-1 events.
- `test_cell_table` reads the newest `depth_cells_2026-11-1[56]*.csv` by sort order; `test_options` reads the 16b tables.
- `pkill -f "python run.py"` also kills the calling shell; kill by PID. `conda run` does not pass a heredoc through stdin.
