## Traps
- A literature class reaches `classify.py` only through a hint row; an A3 match to Ruiz and Madariaga alone does not set it (LEARNINGS).
- Some hint rows cite Ruiz and Madariaga by PDF page ("p. 9", "p. 16"); journal page = PDF page + 36.
- Aysén 1960: every Mw 7.7 is Kanamori and Rivera (2017); the epicentre choice (ISC-GEM, 41 km east of Centennial) is a judgement between two A-quality solutions. Which LOFS branch slipped is unknown (WORKLOG addendum). Kanamori and Rivera copied the Centennial epicentre; they are not a second location.
- NOAA tsunami validity grades the tsunami, not the earthquake; only 3–4 was used, plus 1851-05-26 with Lomnitz as a second report.
- North of 17° S (`DOMAIN` in `locate.py`) southern-Peru giants are classed in-slab by position and are unchecked (1784, 1821, 1600 ×2 near the Huaynaputina eruption); harmless while out of the domain (LEARNINGS).
