Other properties:
- **Location and depth order, all periods where the source exists:** Potin → GCCEL → ISC-EHB L1/L2 → ISC → ComCat → CSN; depth = first measured depth in that order, fixed and default depths avoided wherever a measured one exists; no regional exceptions (**S**, Pablo 11 Nov).

## 3. Coverage of the global catalogues

- **Centennial (Engdahl and Villaseñor 2002):** M ≥ 7.0 before the 1930s, M ≥ 6.5 from the 1930s to 1963, M ≥ 5.5 from 1964.
- **ISC-GEM:** first release cut-offs Ms ≥ 7.5 (1900–1917), 6.25 (1918–1959), 5.5 (from 1960); the extension project added about 12 000 smaller events in 1904–1959. Stated completeness Mw 5.6 from 1964, 6.2–7.5 in the early period.
- Below these limits, before 1982 only the ISC Bulletin (ISS from 1918, ISC from 1964), ComCat and Cabello's historical rows have events.
